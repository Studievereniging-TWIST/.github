## What is TWIST?
TWIST is the study association for linguistics in Leiden. For over twenty years, we’ve been organising activities for our members that allow them to add more depth to their study and meet fellow students along the way. Some of our annual events are the study trip abroad, the TWIST Student Conference, and the Talengala. We also organise lectures, movie nights, game nights, borrels, and much more! Of course, we don’t do all of this by ourselves, but we are assisted by the fantastic members of our committees.

## What is this GitHub organisation for?
TWIST's GitHub organisation is used for various things that require some amount of programming, such as typesetting our quarterly magazine, the TWISTER, or more administrative tools for both the board and committees.
